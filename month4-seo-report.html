Search visibility

Google showed your site 578 times in search this month, with an average position of 39.5. More than half of the last three months' 1,090 impressions came in this single month.

How to read these numbers. Impressions = how many times your pages appeared in Google results. Average position = where your pages rank on average; position 1–10 is page 1, 11–20 is page 2, and so on. Impressions rising and position falling toward 1 are the two signals that come before steady traffic.

Off-page SEO and link building

Your site earned its first guest post backlink from CamoCrypt and a profile backlink from about.me. A second guest post for Crypto Pro Network is written and waiting for your content approval, and outreach emails have gone to five more crypto sites.

Why backlinks matter. Each link from a trusted crypto website acts as a vote of confidence that tells Google your site is reliable. DA / DR (Domain Authority / Domain Rating) score a website's strength from 0 to 100; links from sites scoring 30+ carry real weight for a site at your stage.

Why we chose guest posts over directories. Bulk directory listings mainly help local businesses with a physical address. FreedomCrypto is an online platform, so we put your link budget entirely into quality guest posts on real crypto sites with traffic and authority, where each link does far more for your rankings.
Long-term recommendation. Crypto is one of the most competitive niches in search. To build domain authority meaningfully, we recommend a target of 50–60 guest posts over time, rolled out gradually according to budget and results.
